Our story

Our story

This idea didn't come from a business plan. It came from real life — from watching people in the office every day.

While working in an office, I noticed something simple but powerful: every time people took a 10-minute break, they used it to drink coffee, scroll their phone, or smoke.

Everyone was tired. Everyone was stressed. Everyone was rushing.

But those 10 minutes were the only moments people had for themselves. And I kept thinking — what if those 10 minutes could actually help people feel better? What if a break could reduce stress instead of adding more? What if we could replace a smoke break with a calm break?

They don't need to travel anywhere. They don't need complicated routines. They just need a moment of calm — right where they are.

So I created a service designed exactly for that: short, effective head and foot relaxation sessions that fit into real life. Sessions that work for busy people, office workers, parents, students — anyone who needs a break that actually helps.

Even 10 minutes can change how your whole day feels.

Today, Calm Corner brings that moment of calm to workplaces, events, and individuals across Tampere — helping people feel lighter, clearer, and more connected to themselves.
